# Rule changes v4 (apply on top of v1 + v2 + v3)

All earlier rules still apply except where changed below. Approved by the designer after simulation round 3. This is planned as the final simulation round before the PRD is updated.

## 1. Shield Bash
- Damage 2d6 (was 1d8). Everything else unchanged (Minor, range 1, physical, push 1 on hit).

## 2. Ranged physical damage uses SEN
- Physical attacks made at distance >= 2 add SEN mod to damage instead of POW mod. In the test this affects Long Shot, Pin and Quill Volley. Physical attacks at distance 1 still add POW mod.
- Attack rolls are unchanged (d20 + SEN mod + proficiency).
- Quillfox damage with this rule: 1 star Long Shot 1d8+2, 4 star 1d8+6.

## 3. Burst
- Burst hits at most 2 enemies inside the 3x3 area; the user chooses which.

## 4. Turn-limit tiebreak
- If the battle reaches 10 turns per side, the side that dealt more total damage during the battle wins (healing does not subtract). Equal damage = draw.

## 5. Command vs Tamer protection
- No change: Command's advantage cancels the protection disadvantage under the normal rule. Stated explicitly so it is not ambiguous.

## Rulings carried from round 3
- Shield Bash "directly away" pushes diagonally when the target is diagonally adjacent.
- A Counter triggered by Shield Bash resolves before the push.
- A Pinned unit can still be pushed.
